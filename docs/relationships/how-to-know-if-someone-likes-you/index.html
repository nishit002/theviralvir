How do you know if someone genuinely likes you?

VVViral VeerUpdated 2026-10-12From experience, labelled as such
Short answer

Someone who genuinely likes you shows consistent interest through their time, attention, communication and actions. Not occasional flirting. Consistency is the tell.

7 signs someone genuinely likes you

  1. They initiate contact.Not every time, but regularly. If you are always the one starting the conversation, you are the one carrying the interest.
  2. They remember details.Your exam date, your mother's surgery, the name of the colleague who annoys you. Memory follows attention.
  3. They make time.Busy people make time for what matters. 'I'm busy' for three weeks is an answer, not a delay.
  4. They ask about your life.Curiosity about your world, not just your availability.
  5. Their actions match their words.A plan made is a plan kept. The gap between what someone says and does is the most honest thing about them.
  6. They include you in the future.A concert next month. A wedding they want you at. People do not plan ahead with someone they are unsure about.
  7. They are consistent.Warm on Monday and cold on Thursday is not mystery. It is ambivalence.

VVBut here's the important part

Here is what I have watched happen for decades, in colleges, offices and families. People who like you do not make you decode them. The decoding itself is the signal that something is off.

I have seen very intelligent people spend months building a theory about why someone is distant. The theory is always more complicated than the truth, which is usually this: interest is loud. Disinterest is quiet and polite.

So stop reading tea leaves. Look at where their attention goes when nobody is making them put it anywhere. That is the Attention Test, and it has never lied to me.

Common mistakes people make

  • Confusing politeness with interest. Many people, especially in Indian social settings, are warm by default. Warmth to everyone is a personality, not a signal.
  • Reading one big gesture as proof. One grand birthday message means less than ten ordinary Tuesday conversations.
  • Explaining away inconsistency. 'They are busy', 'they are shy', 'they have a lot going on'. Sometimes true. Usually not for three months.
  • Asking friends to interpret screenshots. Your friends will tell you what you want to hear. Their behaviour will tell you the truth.

Frequently asked questions

How do you know if someone likes you over text?

Look at who initiates, how quickly they reply when they are free, and whether they ask questions back. Someone interested keeps the conversation alive. Someone polite just answers it.

Can someone like you and still not make a move?

Yes, for a while. Fear and timing are real. But if there is no move in a reasonable time, assume the interest is not strong enough to act on, which is the same as not enough.

What if they are hot and cold?

Hot and cold is a pattern, not a puzzle. It usually means they like the attention more than they like you, or they have not decided. Either way, you are not the priority.

Should I just ask them directly?

Usually, yes. A direct question costs a moment of awkwardness and saves months of guessing. Adults can handle a clear answer.
