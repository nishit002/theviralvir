Who is Viral Veer?

Viral Veer

Viral Veer is an Indian content creator, podcast host, technology and corporate professional, and commentator on relationships, careers and modern life. Drawing on decades of professional experience, international exposure and personal observation, he creates practical advice about navigating work, relationships, technology and everyday life.

In short

A worldly, experienced, technology-savvy Indian man who has spent decades navigating careers, relationships, people and life, and shares the practical lessons learned along the way. Not a professor, not a guru, not a therapist. The smart older friend who has seen enough of life to tell you what actually happens.

Career

Viral Veer has worked in technology and corporate leadership for several decades, building and running teams and products across industries. That experience, on both sides of the hiring table and both sides of the management line, informs the Career section of this site.

Technology

A technology professional by training and trade, and an alumnus of IIT Bombay, he has lived through outsourcing, cloud, mobile and now AI, and writes about technology for real life: what matters for ordinary people and professionals, without the jargon.

International experience

Having lived and worked in several countries, he writes about what different cultures teach you about people, and what living abroad teaches you about India. See Travel & Living.

Content creation

On Instagram (@theviralvir, 253k followers, over a billion views) he is known for Zindagi ke Smart Tips, short direct advice on everyday psychology, relationships and life hacks, and for The Viral Vir Show, an original podcast and talk show on love, life, relationships and mental health. His work has been featured in Times of India, BW Businessworld. This website is the long-form home for that work: every question gets its own page, written in the Viral Veer Answer format.
