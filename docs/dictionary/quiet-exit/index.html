What is Quiet Exit?

Definition

Leaving a bad situation on your own terms, without drama, with your record intact.

The opposite of the dramatic resignation or the public breakup. Takes more discipline and leaves fewer scars.

Where it came from

Quiet Exit is a Viral Veer concept: a rule of thumb built from years of watching the same situations play out in offices, families and relationships. It is a lens, not a law.

More concepts

  • The 3C RuleChemistry + Compatibility + Character.
  • The 3R RuleRespect + Reliability + Reciprocity.
  • The Career CeilingThe point where technical competence stops producing career growth.
  • The Attention TestWhere someone's attention goes when nobody is directing it reveals their priorities.
  • The Exit TestA relationship, job or situation has reached its natural end when you are staying for reasons that have nothing to do with it.
  • Rented FriendshipsFriendships held together by circumstance rather than character.
  • The Tuesday TestJudge a relationship by an ordinary Tuesday, not by birthdays and holidays.
