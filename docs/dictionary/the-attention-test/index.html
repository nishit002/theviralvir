What is The Attention Test?

Definition

Where someone's attention goes when nobody is directing it reveals their priorities.

People can say anything. Attention is expensive and finite, so where it goes is honest. Works for partners, bosses and friends.

Where it came from

The Attention Test is a Viral Veer concept: a rule of thumb built from years of watching the same situations play out in offices, families and relationships. It is a lens, not a law.

More concepts

  • The 3C RuleChemistry + Compatibility + Character.
  • The 3R RuleRespect + Reliability + Reciprocity.
  • The Career CeilingThe point where technical competence stops producing career growth.
  • The Exit TestA relationship, job or situation has reached its natural end when you are staying for reasons that have nothing to do with it.
  • Rented FriendshipsFriendships held together by circumstance rather than character.
  • The Tuesday TestJudge a relationship by an ordinary Tuesday, not by birthdays and holidays.
  • Quiet ExitLeaving a bad situation on your own terms, without drama, with your record intact.
