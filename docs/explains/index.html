Why people do what they do. One honest explanation per page, from someone who has watched the same patterns repeat for decades.

Viral Veer Explains

Why people ghost

Ghosting is rarely about you. It is about the ghoster's inability to handle a small, uncomfortable conversation. Avoidance is a habit, not a verdict.

Viral Veer Explains

Why people cheat

Opportunity, unmet needs and low character, in varying mixes. Most cheating starts as an ego fix, not a love story.

Viral Veer Explains

Why people fall out of love

Love does not usually die in a fight. It dies in a thousand ordinary evenings where nobody made an effort.

Viral Veer Explains

Why smart people procrastinate

Smart people can see all the ways a task could go wrong, so they delay starting. Perfectionism dressed up as planning.

Viral Veer Explains

Why office politics exists

Because organisations have limited rewards and unlimited ambition. Politics is how humans allocate scarce things. You can refuse to play, but you cannot refuse to be affected.

Viral Veer Explains

Why people hate their jobs

Usually three things: no control, no progress, or a bad manager. Pay is rarely the first reason, though it is the most common excuse.

Viral Veer Explains

Why confidence is attractive

Confidence signals that someone can handle themselves, so you will not have to. It is attractive for the same reason competence is.

Viral Veer Explains

Why money does not always make people happy

Money removes certain miseries. It does not add meaning. People who expect it to do both are disappointed on schedule.
